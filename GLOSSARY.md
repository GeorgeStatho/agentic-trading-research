# Glossary

Domain terms used in code, docs and architecture reviews.

- **Frozen payload**: the exact input a stage decided on, saved at decision time so the decision can be replayed later. The manager's is `manager_decision_payloads.replay_input` (`manager_replay_payload`; equal to the `pre_manager` checkpoint when the decision has a candidate). The strategist's is the `pre_strategist` checkpoint.
- **Frozen decision**: one manager decision's Frozen payloads, plus the bucket chain production selected from (the `pre_contract_selection` checkpoint) and the candidate's pipeline manifest. Replays read them only through `FrozenDecision` (`llm_replay_store.py`), which hash-checks every checkpoint; they never query those stores directly.
- **Stage decision**: frozen payload → recommendation. `decide_manager_recommendation` and `decide_strategist_recommendation` run it identically for live runs and for replays (LLM backtest, challenger and offline evaluation); live adds payload building, persistence and gates around it.
- **Evidence quality**: `strong`, `adequate`, `weak` or `unchecked`: how well a decision's thesis was grounded. A Phase 4 thesis check decides it when one exists; otherwise a deterministic proxy over the cited articles and primary catalyst does. The strategist's own `evidence_quality` rating is recorded beside it, never used to compute it.
- **Direction outcome**: `correct`, `wrong`, `flat`, `abstained`, `pending` or `unavailable`: whether the underlying moved as the call said, from the frozen reference price to the close 5 (primary) and 10 trading days later. A move within ±0.5% is `flat`.
- **Analyst consensus**: Yahoo's estimate for a company's next report, one per metric, labelled `next_report:<D>` with D the New York date of that report. A capture taken on or after D is stale (Yahoo has already moved on to the next quarter under D's label). An estimate matches only the quarter reported on D, and is collected only for companies that report in USD. `services/discovery/analyst_consensus.py` owns all of it.
- **Primary failure**: `evidence`, `forecast`, `expression`, `none` or `pending`: the first layer that failed. Weak evidence with a wrong direction is `evidence`, better evidence with a wrong direction is `forecast`, a right direction with a losing trade is `expression`.
